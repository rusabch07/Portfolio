GL3 Burner Automation

Overview

A PLC/HMI-based industrial control project designed around the safe and observable operation of a GL3 burner.

Problem

Solution

A staged control sequence covering purge, fuel validation, ignition, flame confirmation and continued monitoring.

Features

  • Purge sequence
  • Fuel checking and ignition
  • Flame detection
  • VFD-controlled fans
  • Temperature monitoring
